1. Size up the reference

The thing on the left is shown at its real height: a person, a door, the Statue of Liberty. Every round asks one question: how tall is the other one?

2. Resize the right one

Drag the picture or the slider until it looks right. Use ×10 and ÷10 for big jumps. Lock in within 20 seconds.

3. Watch it grow

Your guess stays as a ghost while the real size grows into place, next to everyone else's guesses. Ten rounds, with a surprise or two.
