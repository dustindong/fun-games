1. Read the reference

The object on the left is shown at its real height, like a compact car at 4 ft 9 in tall.

2. Resize the right one

Drag the slider or the picture to make it taller or shorter. Use ×10 and ÷10 for big jumps.

3. Lock in by 20 s

Ten rounds per game. Everyone in the room gets the same pairs.
