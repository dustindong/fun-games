## Expanded regular-pool items

Bowling pin, traffic cone, carry-on suitcase, wine bottle, office desk, washing machine, kitchen counter, ping-pong table, hockey goal, track hurdle, tennis net, balance beam, 9-volt battery, AAA battery, D battery, paperback book, die, ping-pong ball, coffee mug, soda can.

These reuse existing catalog artwork and height conventions. Some already appeared in hand-picked rounds; standardizing them adds many more compatible pairings. Generic household objects are explicitly labeled representative examples, not universal dimensions. A tennis net is measured at the posts, a table at its playing surface, and a suitcase with its handle retracted. Do not change these to length comparisons.

## Session rules

- Completed scored matches are captured once by game ID in the host's room state.
- Wins carry across rematches within that room and are broadcast to guests.
- Every tied top scorer earns a win, provided there are at least two players and a positive top score.
- Practice, unfinished games, and solo games do not award wins.
- Departed players retain previously earned wins; names update when the same player ID rejoins.
- A new room starts a new session. This is not a cross-room or persistent account leaderboard.
- Closest guess uses unrounded relative error. Biggest overestimate excludes misses and differences under 0.5%.
- Biggest comeback is the largest recovery from an earlier rank to final rank, with a real comparison from the recovery period. No recovery means no comeback award.
