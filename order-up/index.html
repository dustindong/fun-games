1. Get five things

Everyone gets the same five things and one question, like Pixar movies, oldest to newest. You'll recognize them all. Probably.

2. Sort the list

Drag the rows or use the arrows. Each round tells you what goes on top, like biggest first or oldest first.

3. Lock in, then argue

You have 45 seconds. Then the right order slides in and you see where everyone else put things. Ten rounds, highest total wins.
